Services

Six trades, one contractor

The services we undertake are set out below, each with its usual scope of works.

Plumbing & water

Supply, waste and storage, from repairs to new installations.

Plumbing repairs

Leaks, blockages and faulty taps, valves and cisterns repaired, with damaged pipework replaced.

New installations

Supply and waste pipework installed for new bathrooms, kitchens and extensions.

Water tanks & pumps

Storage tanks installed on a prepared base and connected, with pressure pumps fitted.

Prepaid sub-meters

Prepaid water sub-meters supplied and fitted, so that each unit is metered separately.

Hot water & energy

Geysers, gas water heaters, solar panels and backup power.

Gas water heaters

Gas water heaters installed and connected to the supply, with the hot water pipework completed.

Geyser installation

Geysers installed and replaced, together with the valves, drip trays and connections.

Solar panels

Mounting frames fixed to the roof and solar panels installed and connected.

Inverters & batteries

Inverter and battery systems installed, with protection boards, for backup power.

Renovations

Turnkey renovations and upgrades, completed under a single instruction.

Bathroom renovations

Bathrooms stripped out and rebuilt, from the first pipe run to the final fitting.

Tiling

Large-format wall and floor tiles laid on prepared surfaces, and grouted and sealed.

Vanities & sanitaryware

Vanities, basins, baths, showers and toilets installed and connected.

Turnkey renovations

Renovations and upgrades taken from strip-out to completion by our own team.

Process

How a project is carried out

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Contact us by telephone or WhatsApp with a description of the work required and the property address.

  2. 02

    Site assessment

    We attend the property to assess the work and take measurements.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    We issue a quotation setting out the works, materials and labour.

  4. 04

    Works & handover

    We carry out the works in sequence, and test and inspect the completed installation with you.
