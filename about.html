The company

Plumbing and renovation contractors

MTP Trading & Projects is based on the corner of 3rd Avenue and Goldman Street in Florida, Roodepoort. We specialise in turnkey renovations, upgrades and new installations.

Our work covers plumbing, bathrooms and tiling, geysers and gas water heaters, solar panels and backup power, water storage and prepaid sub-metering.

Every project is carried through to a finished standard, with the installation tested and inspected with the customer on completion.
