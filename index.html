Bathroom renovations

A large part of our work is the renovation of bathrooms, carried out turnkey: from stripping out the existing room to the final fitting of sanitaryware.

Plumbing, tiling and fitting are undertaken by our own team, so that a bathroom is completed under a single instruction.

Plumbing — supply and waste pipework rerouted where the layout changes
Tiling — large-format wall and floor tiles laid
Showers — glass enclosures and screens installed
Fittings — baths, vanities, toilets and mixers fitted and tested
